#### B. Acoustic Environment Monitoring & Studio Mode (`voice/src/audio/`)
- **`EnvironmentMonitor` (`environmentMonitor.ts`):** Monitors acoustic room conditions (`quiet`, `moderate`, `noisy`) by measuring median RMS noise floors specifically during VAD `IDLE` periods (`IDLE_RING_SIZE = 250`), avoiding speech contamination.
- **Studio Mode Toggle (`captureAdapter.ts`, `voicePipeline.ts`):** Allows users in quiet rooms with headsets to disable browser-level DSP (`echoCancellation: false`, `noiseSuppression: false`, `autoGainControl: false`) and automatically adjust VAD sensitivity (`speechStartThreshold: 0.65 vs 0.70`), preserving pristine raw speech dynamics.
- **Smart UX Toasts & Phantom-Utterance Defense:** Evaluates recent `stt.final` results over a 2-minute sliding window (`STT_WINDOW_MS`). If empty or low-confidence finals exceed 40% (`STT_BAD_RATIO`), or if room noise levels shift significantly, the UI prompts a toast suggesting the user toggle Studio Mode on/off.

#### C. STT Hallucination Defense & Pre-flight Gate (`stt/stt_service/service.py`)
- **Audio Pre-processing Pipeline:** Added `_highpass_filter() -> _normalize_audio() -> _trim_trailing_silence()` prior to feeding audio buffers into Whisper ASR.
- **Silence Pre-flight Guard:** Implemented `_is_silence(decode_audio)` right after normalization. If the signal is dead air or pure background silence, ASR transcription is skipped entirely (`silence, skipped ASR`). This saves GPU inference costs and prevents Whisper from hallucinating spurious text (`"Cảm ơn các bạn đã theo dõi"`, etc.).
